## Information we collect

When you submit a lead form (for example, through a Facebook or Instagram Lead
Ad) or otherwise contact us, we may collect:

- **Contact details** — name, email address, phone number.
- **Professional details** — job title, company/organization.
- **Lead source / advertising attribution** — the platform, campaign, ad set,
  ad, and form your inquiry came from.
- **Engagement records** — notes, status, follow-up dates, and activity we log
  while working your inquiry (calls, emails, meetings).
- **Payment confirmation** — if you become a customer, transaction metadata
  (amount, date, processor identifiers). Card details are handled by our payment
  processor; we do not store full card numbers.

We collect this information directly from you, and from the advertising and
service platforms listed below when you interact with our ads or communications.

- To respond to your inquiry and provide the products or services you requested.

- To process payments and maintain customer records.
- To measure advertising performance (e.g., cost per lead) in aggregate.
- To comply with legal obligations.

We rely on your consent (submitting the form), our legitimate business interest
in responding to inquiries, and, where applicable, performance of a contract.
